Ripple: Golf Shot Tracker Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 20, 2026

Ripple: Golf Shot Tracker is designed to keep your golf data on your devices.

Ripple does not collect, sell, or share personal information with the developer or third-party advertising or analytics services.

Information you enter into Ripple, including golf rounds, shots, club information, course names, settings, and statistics, is stored locally on your device. When you use the Apple Watch companion app, golf data may be transferred between your paired Apple Watch and iPhone using Apple's device communication technologies.

Ripple can create data exports at your request. Those exports remain under your control and are only sent to another destination when you choose to share or save them using Apple's system sharing features.

Ripple does not require an account and does not contain third-party advertising or tracking.

Apple may independently process information relating to App Store downloads, purchases, TestFlight, or Apple services according to Apple's own privacy policies.
